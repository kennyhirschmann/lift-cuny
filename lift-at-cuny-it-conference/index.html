About the LIFT@CUNY Collective

LIFT@CUNY is a grassroots, cross-campus community of practice advancing learning design, innovation, and flexible technology in teaching and learning.

We bring together instructional designers, educational technologists, online learning specialists, LMS administrators, and faculty partners to exchange expertise, solve shared challenges, and explore emerging practices.

Connect & share

Build relationships across campuses, exchange ideas, and learn from successes and challenges.

Explore emerging technologies

Discuss and investigate AI, XR/VR, HyFlex, and other evolving teaching and learning practices.

Collaborate across campuses

Work together on practical solutions, open resources, and opportunities that benefit the wider CUNY community.
