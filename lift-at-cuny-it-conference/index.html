ABOUT THE COLLECTIVE

Who We Are

LIFT@CUNY Collective is a grassroots community created by and for professional staff across CUNY, but everyone is welcome. We bring together people who are interested in how technology can support teaching and learning, and who believe there's value in learning from and with one another.

It's a space to exchange ideas, share experiences, explore new approaches, and work together on common challenges and opportunities across our campuses.

Share ideas. Try things. Learn together.

Connect & share

Meet colleagues across campuses, exchange ideas, and learn from different perspectives.

Explore & experiment

Consider emerging tools and practices, including AI, HyFlex, accessibility, and more.

Collaborate & research

Work together on shared challenges, professional learning, and collaborative inquiry.
